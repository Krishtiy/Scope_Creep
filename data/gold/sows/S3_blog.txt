Blog Content Package — Statement of Work

Project: Monthly blog content for TechStart Inc.
Deliverables:
- 4 blog posts per month (800–1,000 words each)
- Topics agreed in advance via email
- One round of revisions per post

Revision definition: One revision round = client submits all feedback in a single email
within 5 business days of delivery. Feedback received after 5 days or in multiple emails
counts as a new revision round.

Excluded:
- Social media captions
- Email newsletters
- Video scripts
- SEO keyword research
- Graphic design or images

Timeline: Posts delivered by the 15th of each month.
Payment: $400/month, invoiced on the 1st, due within 14 days.
Client responsibilities: Provide topic brief and any required facts/stats by the 5th of each month.
